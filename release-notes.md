**Real hard drive sounds for your Mac.** Flip one switch in your menu bar and your silent SSD sounds like a mechanical drive again, driven by what your Mac is actually reading and writing.

* Six drives: 3.5″ floppy, 5.25″ floppy, 1986 MFM stepper, 1995 IDE, 7200 RPM desktop, 15K RPM server
* Live mode follows real disk activity; Ambient and Busy simulate it
* Menu bar activity light: green for reads, amber for writes
* Spin up, calibration and head parking
* Universal app: Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 13 or later

**Install:** download **Platter.zip**, unzip, drag Platter into Applications and open it. If macOS says it can't verify the developer, go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and click **Open Anyway**, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Platter.app`.

Watch the demo: https://hurtz.github.io/platter/
